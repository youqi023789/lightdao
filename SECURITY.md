# LightDAO Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please report security issues responsibly. Do NOT open public issues for security bugs.
- Email: security@lightdao.net
- Or GitHub private advisory: https://github.com/youqi023789/lightdao/security/advisories

## Scope
In-scope: core chain (x/lightfee), the 10 CosmWasm contracts, gateway scoring/Merkle proofing, client wallet (Passkey/Shamir), relay/signaling infra.
Out-of-scope: social engineering, physical access, third-party CDN/dependencies, testnet-only DoS.

## Disclosure timeline
- We acknowledge within 48h.
- 90-day coordinated disclosure window; we fix and publish, then credit you (unless you prefer anonymous).

## Bug bounty
Up to USD 50,000, tiered by severity (critical/high/medium/low), paid in LIGHT or fiat at our discretion once a liquid market exists.

## Safe harbor
Good-faith research following this policy is authorized. We will not pursue legal action against good-faith reporters.
