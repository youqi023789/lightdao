Privacy Policy 隐私政策

LightDAO is a self-custodial, browser-native protocol. We minimize data by design.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, no emails required to mine (newsletter is opt-in).
  • No private keys, seeds, or Passkey credentials ever leave your device.
  • No third-party ad trackers or analytics beacons on the client.

What the network necessarily sees

  • Your wallet address and on-chain transactions (public by nature of a blockchain).
  • Mining contribution metrics you broadcast (bandwidth/uptime/verification/stability) and an anti-sybil device fingerprint hash + IP, used only to score contribution and prevent fraud.
  • Standard server access logs (IP, UA) for security and rate-limiting.

Your controls

  • Stop mining any time; contribution broadcasting stops.
  • Clear browser storage to remove local wallet session & referral data.
  • Request log deletion: privacy@lightdao.net.

Cookies / local storage

We use localStorage/sessionStorage for wallet session, language, and referral attribution only. No cross-site cookies.
